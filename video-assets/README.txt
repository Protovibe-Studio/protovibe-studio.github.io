PROTOVIBE STUDIO — MOTION ASSETS

Open either HTML file directly in a browser. Each is completely self-contained,
works offline, and can be moved out of this repository by itself.

logo-reveal.html: five-second single take ending on darkness.
logo-loop.html: the same five-second sequence, repeating continuously.

TIMING
0.12–2.17 s: a stationary oval spotlight centered on the P grows to reveal
the whole wordmark; its soft feather widens as it expands. Global logo opacity
warms from 70% to 100% across the reveal, with a brief dark-opening fade.
0.12–2.17 s: blue and violet/pink begin together with the logo, using a sine
ease-in/out; both reach full brightness when the logo is fully revealed.
Throughout the take, blue drifts right and violet left while both clouds expand,
softly stretch, and rotate with offset sine ease-in/out curves. Motion continues
through the fade; there is no static hold.
3.55–4.65 s: background lights fade out.
3.65–4.75 s: logo fades out with the same easing, trailing by 100 ms.
4.75–5.0 s: dark tail, then stop or repeat.

RECORDING
Use a 1920 × 1080 or 3840 × 2160 display/capture area, enter fullscreen (F),
start your screen recorder, then press R to replay from darkness.
R = replay; Space = pause/play; F = fullscreen; H = show/hide controls.
Double-click also toggles controls. Controls and cursor are hidden by default.
When controls are shown, the slider lets you inspect any moment.
Optional URL suffix ?t=4 opens on a paused frame at 4 seconds.

SOURCE
Background #070707 and the blue/violet/rose OKLCH colors come from the landing page.
The full Studio wordmark available in the repo is a 606 × 72 transparent PNG,
embedded unchanged. No full-wordmark SVG was found; public/icon.svg is the P icon
only. The PNG may look soft in very large captures; a vector original would improve
4K sharpness. Neither file depends on the app, a server, fonts, or a package install.
